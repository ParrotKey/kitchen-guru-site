Houston FAQ

Simple answers before you join.

These answers stay intentionally practical: what the app does, what it does not expose, and how court-specific alerts work.

How does Kitchen Guru help pickleball players in Houston?

Kitchen Guru helps Houston players follow saved courts, check anonymous court activity, and connect with players they actually meet at public courts. It is built for the local problem that distance and traffic make court-level updates more valuable than general nearby chatter.

Can I choose which Houston courts send alerts?

Yes. The alert model is court-specific: save the courts you actually play, then use those saved courts for future player-needed and court-update alerts.

Will strangers see my name or exact location?

No. The first layer is anonymous court activity. Names, presence, and direct messages are for mutual friends after both players approve the connection.

Is Kitchen Guru free to join?

The public pages are collecting beta interest. The app starts with a free account path, while Kitchen Guru+ features remain part of the controlled release plan.

Who is Kitchen Guru for?

Kitchen Guru is intended for adults 18 and older who meet at public pickleball courts, want less guessing before they leave, and prefer simple court-level alerts instead of noisy group chats.
