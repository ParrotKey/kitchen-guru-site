Pickup play, made visible

Know before you make the drive.

Kitchen Guru turns the scattered signals around local pickleball into one simple rhythm: check the courts, help the next player, and keep playing with the people you meet.

01 / FIND

Find the court with a game.

Open the map and see fresh activity before you load the paddle bag. Kitchen Guru shows where a game may be forming without turning the court into a public roster.

See court activity while player identities stay private
02 / KNOW

Know what is happening at the court.

Check the latest Court Pulse, see how many players are there, and spot requests for another player. Save the courts that matter so the rest of the city stays quiet.

Useful updates from the people who are actually there
03 / CONNECT

Keep the people who made it fun.

When two players choose to connect, Kitchen Guru gives them a private place to arrange the rematch. No giant group text and no public location trail.

Both players accept before a conversation starts
